Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Hestora Limited uses cookies and similar technologies on hestora.net. For how we handle personal data more generally, see our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as your browser's local storage) work in the same way. They help websites function, remember your choices, and understand how the site is used.
