How it works

Three things happen. You just enjoy the savings.

Hestora manages everything in the background. All you notice is the same warm home and a lower bill.

01

Charges overnight

From midnight, Hestora fills up on cheap off-peak electricity, automatically finding the cheapest window so you don't have to.

Water is heated to up to 175°C inside a pressurised vessel rated to PD 5500:2024+A2 (Category 2), independently tested to 17.86 bar by British Engineering Services, over double its 8 bar operating pressure. Round-trip efficiency ~92%; standing loss ~4 kWh/day, largely recovered as useful space heat.
02

Delivers on demand

Stored heat flows to your radiators and hot water taps when you need it, just like a boiler, but the energy was bought overnight when it was cheap.

Heat exchangers deliver mains-pressure domestic hot water and feed a conventional wet central-heating circuit. No emitter upgrades, no new pipework, no outdoor unit. The vessel arrives light and is filled with water on-site, installed in a single day.
03

Earns on the grid

From cheap overnight power to surges in daytime solar and wind, Hestora charges whenever energy is cheapest or greenest.

NESO's Demand Flexibility Service pays controllable demand assets to absorb surplus renewable electricity. Each Hestora unit can absorb 40 kWh on instruction.

Hestora has a compact 660 mm × 660 mm footprint—similar to a typical white good or hot water cylinder. It arrives lightweight and is filled with water on-site, making it easy to move through hallways and up stairs. It is suitable for houses, bungalows and maisonettes with a wet central heating system.
It’s built to strict UK engineering standards and independently tested by British Engineering Services. They tested the pressure tank at more than double its maximum daily working pressure, so you can be completely confident in its safety and build quality.

No. Hestora works with whatever heat emitters you already have: radiators or underfloor heating.
